One order.
Smaller jobs.

Design a program that takes a concession order and produces an itemized receipt. Find the responsibilities, name the smaller jobs, and follow the information between them.

1

Investigate a missing job

This first design has three jobs. Choose which ones to include and watch what happens.

  1. 1 · Get the order

    Needs: customer choices

    Included · not tested

  2. 2 · Calculate the total

    Needs: order and prices

    Omitted from this design

  3. 3 · Make the receipt

    Needs: order and total

    Included · not tested

Test once, change one choice, then test again. Compare the result with your prediction.

2

Build your own smaller jobs

Here are six responsibilities. Give your jobs names, then decide which responsibilities belong together. Use two or three groups.

Different groupings can work. Aim for one clear purpose in each group. The groups describe who does what; they do not set the order in which tasks run.

3

Follow the data and test your design

Each responsibility has a built-in action. The test follows their data dependencies and labels each action with your group. You can see missing work even before your design is complete.

Expected total: $10.00

Assign some responsibilities above, then test an order.

    Customer’s receipt

    A complete receipt checks task coverage. You still need to explain whether each group has a clear purpose. This activity tests valid, non-negative quantities; input validation can be a further responsibility.

    4

    Explain your decomposition

    Use the trace as evidence. You may revise your groups and test again.

    Connect this to Python

    A smaller job with a clear purpose may become a function. Identify its inputs, its work and its returned result. Your decomposition groups do not automatically become functions: first check that each group has a coherent responsibility and that you can explain its data dependencies.

    For example, a calculation function could take an order and prices and return the total. The main program can coordinate getting the order, calling the calculation and displaying the receipt.
