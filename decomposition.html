Decomposition

Divide a larger problem into smaller jobs with clear responsibilities. Work out what each job needs, what it produces, and how it connects to the others.

One problem. Smaller responsibilities.

A concession receipt needs an order, a calculation and a way to show the result. Here is one possible split:

Get the orderCustomer choices → order
Calculate the totalOrder and prices → total
Make the receiptOrder and total → receipt

This is one possible decomposition. Other groupings can work when their responsibilities and data connections are clear.
